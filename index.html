Some days don't go according to plan. Some make us cry before they make us smile. And some quietly teach us to trust Allah when we cannot see what comes next.

01

THE MORNING

10:00 AM

I thought I was ready.

The competition was supposed to start at 10 in the morning. I went there ready to log in, expecting to find my personal link waiting for me.

I had prepared for this moment and was looking forward to finally taking the competition.

02

THE PROBLEM

“We couldn't find your registration.”

Those words changed everything.

For a moment, it felt like all the effort I had put into preparing had disappeared.
03

THE MOMENT IT HURT

I cried.

I cried a lot.

I started thinking about all the time I had spent preparing and wondering whether it had all been wasted.

I was supposed to be preparing for JAMB too, so the thought of losing this opportunity hurt even more.

THE DUʿĀʾ I HELD ON TO

✦
اللهم لا سهل إلا ما جعلته سهلا، وأنت تجعل الحزن إذا شئت سهلا

Allahumma lā sahla illā mā jaʿaltahu sahlan, wa anta tajʿalul-ḥazna idhā shi’ta sahlan.

“O Allah, there is no ease except in what You have made easy, and You can make difficulty easy if You will.”

I didn't know how the situation would be resolved. I only knew who I could turn to.

04

WHAT I WAS TOLD

“Maybe next year.”

One of the coordinators told me that they could not find my registration and that I might have to join the next edition.

I said, “Okay, no problem.”

But inside, I was still hurting.

05

THEN SOMEONE UNDERSTOOD

Someone listened.

I contacted the coordinator in my local government area and explained what had happened.

He understood how much the competition meant to me and later messaged me, asking me to send my details again so they could work towards fixing the problem.

06

THE MESSAGE

✓

“Is this personal link correct?”

Yes. It was correct.
And finally, I could compete.
07

FINALLY

I was back in.

After all the uncertainty, I finally got the chance to take the competition.

I started answering the questions and kept going. I was almost done.

08

ONE LAST OBSTACLE

Then my screen changed.

An app suddenly appeared over the screen and took me away from where I was during the competition.

I had to go back and start that part again.

↻ Starting that part again...

AFTER EVERYTHING

The result?

0 /25

Alhamdulillah.

✦
OVERALL PLACEMENT TOP 11

BUT HERE'S THE TRUTH

I wanted first place.

And honestly, I still do.

I have always tried to strive for the best, and I still hope that one day, In shā Allah, I will become the winner.

24/25 can be a result.
It doesn't have to be the end of the journey.

WHAT I CHOOSE TO REMEMBER

Alhamdulillah.

I cried because I thought I had lost my chance. I prayed because I didn't know what would happen. Someone helped me when I needed it. And eventually, the door opened.

I didn't get the first place I was hoping for. But I got the opportunity to compete, finished with 24 out of 25, and placed among the top 11.

The story didn't end when my registration disappeared.
It continued.

Bismillah for what comes next.

Ameen.
